Description

66 Knockmore (approx. 99 sqm / 1,065 sq ft) is a well-presented three-bedroom semi-detached home situated in the popular Knockmore development. The property offers spacious and practical accommodation throughout, together with excellent access to the front and rear of the property, including an electric front gate and large gated side access.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming entrance hall with guest WC, spacious kitchen, separate utility room with side access, and a bright living room featuring a bay window, fireplace and open fire. Upstairs are three bedrooms, with the main bedroom benefiting from a built-in wardrobe and en-suite, together with two further bedrooms and a family bathroom.

Outside, the property benefits from off-street parking to the front, an electric front gate and large gated side access providing excellent convenience and access to the rear garden. The property also benefits from gas-fired central heating and a C BER rating. Located in the sought-after Knockmore development, the property is within easy reach of schools, shops, transport links, and the M11 motorway.
